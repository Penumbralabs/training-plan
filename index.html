Hunt-Day Safety Adjustments (Mandatory)

These are non-negotiable. You will arrive with less eccentric conditioning than the original 9-week plan intended. These adjustments prevent injury on the mountain.

1. Pack-Out Strategy: 3 Loads, Not 2

Original plan assumed full eccentric conditioning for 100 lbs on a long steep descent. You won't have that. Plan for:

LoadWeightContents
Trip 1~70 lbsGear (30 lbs) + first meat load (40 lbs)
Trip 2~50-60 lbsRemaining meat
Trip 3 (if needed)~30-40 lbsFinal meat/parts

One extra half-day trip is far cheaper than blown quads, a fall, or a knee injury miles from the trailhead.

2. Trekking Poles

Mandatory on every descent, loaded or not. Non-negotiable. Practice technique under load during Weeks 6-7.

3. Descent Technique

  • Rest-step on descents: Short steps, slight pause at full knee extension. Slower but saves quads.
  • Never bomb downhill. Controlled pace. Let time be the buffer.
  • Switchback when possible rather than straight-line descents on steep grades.

4. First 48 Hours in Camp

Don't hunt aggressively Days 1-2. Your legs will be fatigued from the pack-in. Use the first day to:

  • Set up camp properly
  • Scout from camp with glass (binos/spotting scope)
  • Short scouting walks (<3 miles) to locate sign and pattern deer
  • Let legs recover before committing to a stalk that could put you miles from camp

5. Anti-Inflammatory Protocol

  • Ibuprofen 400mg before the big pack-out descent (not daily use, just the critical load)
  • Elevate and stretch legs each evening in camp
  • Stay hydrated (0.5-1L/hr on loaded efforts)

Gear Integration Checklist

  • Actual hunt boots on ALL weighted hikes from Week 5 onward
  • Identify and treat hot spots with leukotape before they become blisters
  • Pack frame loaded to 70-80 lbs (dress rehearsal). Test comfort, adjust fit.
  • Trekking poles. Practice technique under heavy load on descents.
  • Bivy setup/teardown timed and practiced on Week 7 dress rehearsal
  • Hunt nutrition strategy tested on long efforts (what, how much, when)
  • Water filtering tested and practiced on route
  • Game bags, citric acid spray, knife kit. Weight accounted for in pack-out load.

Nutrition Notes

  • Training days: eat to fuel. Don't cut calories during these 5 weeks.
  • Saturday long efforts: practice actual hunt food (bars, jerky, trail mix, freeze-dried)
  • Hydration: target 0.5-1L per hour on loaded efforts. Know your sweat rate.
  • 6 days of food = ~12 lbs (2 lbs/day calorie-dense). Test your menu on Week 6-7 simulations.

Key Principles

  1. Zone 1-2 is king — 80% of volume at conversational pace. Build the aerobic base.
  2. Specificity matters — weighted uphill hiking beats flat running for this objective.
  3. Progressive overload — add load and volume gradually. Even compressed, don't jump ahead.
  4. Recovery is training — adaptation happens during rest, not during the session.
  5. Train the descent — eccentric loading is the most neglected and most punishing aspect.
  6. Respect the gap — you lost 2 weeks. Hunt-day adjustments exist because of this. Follow them.
