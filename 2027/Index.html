Reference: Knee Management Protocol

Daily (Transition through Base 1):

  • Terminal knee extensions (TKE) with band: 3x15
  • Spanish squats: 2x30s
  • Wall sits at 70°: 2x30s

Ongoing rule: If step-ups at 24" cause pain, stay at 18" and progress load instead of height. Sharp pain that increases during session = stop. Dull ache that fades after warmup = proceed.

Red flag: If pain persists past Week 8, get assessed by a physio.

Reference: AeT Heart Rate Drift Test Protocol

  1. No food 60 min before. No caffeine 4 hrs before.
  2. Warm up 15 min easy.
  3. 60 min steady effort at constant pace on flat/consistent surface.
  4. Compare avg HR of first 30 min vs last 30 min.
  5. <5% drift = you were below AeT. >5% = above AeT.
  6. Repeat at different efforts to bracket exact AeT HR.

Schedule: Test at Week 5 (baseline), Week 10, Week 26. Update training zones after each test.

Reference: Key Principles

  1. Zone 1-2 is king — 80% of volume at conversational pace. Build the aerobic base.
  2. Specificity matters — weighted uphill hiking beats flat running for this objective.
  3. Progressive overload — add load and volume gradually. Respect the 10% rule.
  4. Recovery is training — adaptation happens during rest. Step-back weeks are mandatory.
  5. Train the descent — eccentric loading is the most neglected and most punishing aspect.
  6. Max strength first, endurance second — higher ceiling = lower relative effort at any given load.
  7. Simulate the objective — do the hunt in training before you do it for real.
  8. Decision gates exist for a reason — if you fail a gate, extend the phase. Do not skip ahead.
